The second tale begins here.

The city lights flickered below them like dying embers. 

"We shouldn't be here," he whispered, though he made no move to leave.

She smiled, a sharp, dangerous thing. "We shouldn't be anywhere else."

They watched the world move without them, a clockwork machine of steel and glass. 

In this moment, they were the only things that felt real.

The wind carried the sound of distant sirens, but here, high above the noise, there was only the sound of their breathing.

It was a dark and stormy night, but inside the library, the air was thick with the scent of old paper and vanilla. 

He turned the page, his fingers brushing against the rough texture of the parchment. 

"Are you still reading?" she asked, leaning against the doorframe.

He didn't look up. "I'm always reading."

The silence that followed was comfortable, a shared understanding between two souls who found more life in ink than in breath.

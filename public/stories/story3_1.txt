The sun rose over the ruins of the old world.

It was a quiet morning, the kind that made you forget what had been lost.

He sat on the edge of the crater, kicking his feet over the abyss.

"Do you think they'll come back?" he asked the empty air.

The only answer was the rustle of dry grass.

He sighed, picking up a rusted gear and turning it over in his hands.

"Maybe it's better this way," he murmured. "A fresh start."

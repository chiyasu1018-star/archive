A third journey into the unknown.

The forest was alive with whispers. 

Not the kind made by wind in the leaves, but the kind that came from the shadows between the trees.

"Don't listen to them," the guide warned, his hand tight on the hilt of his sword.

But it was too late. The song had already taken root in her mind.

A melody of lost things and forgotten names.

She stepped off the path, and the shadows reached out to welcome her home.
